Who We Are

The Junior Philippine Computer Society (JPCS) at Pateros Technological College is a student organization for students interested in computing, technology, and related activities.

Our Purpose

JPCS - PTC encourages students to develop their knowledge and skills through learning, collaboration, participation, and technology-related activities.
